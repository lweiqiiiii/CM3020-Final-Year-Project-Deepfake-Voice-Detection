For researchers codec test and benchmark results

Codec gauntlet

Re-encodes the current recording through four telephone and VoIP codecs (G.711, G.726, MP3 32 kb/s, Opus 16 kb/s) and scores every version, to see whether the decision survives the channel a scam call would pass through.

Cross-validated codec study

Stratified 5-fold cross-validation on 19 real / 19 fake recordings — every score comes from a model that never saw the file. Bars show how far each codec drags the mean fake score down from the clean-audio baseline.
